# Maintainers

Last reviewed: 2026-10-06.

Who holds which role in Kotiko. The roles and how people join or leave them are in
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md). Changes to this file are made by pull request. The active
maintainers below are exactly the repository's collaborators with the Maintain role (the lead
maintainer is its admin), and `.github/CODEOWNERS` names them. Adding or removing a
maintainer changes this file, the repository's collaborators and CODEOWNERS in the same
step.
