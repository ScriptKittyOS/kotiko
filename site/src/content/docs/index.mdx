Kotiko is a browser extension. You read the news, a recipe or a forum as you always do, and
here and there a word you chose appears in the language you're learning: perro, 猫, спасибо.
Point at it, or tap it, to see what it means in your language and how it's said.

It works on pages in the languages you read, whichever they are. A reader in Madrid sees
"perro" become 犬 on Spanish pages; a reader in Toronto sees "dog" become 犬 on English pages.
Kotiko never assumes you read English.

## Private by design

- The text of the pages you read never leaves your browser.
- A word you add goes only to the AI service you chose, or to your own server, and, for its
  pronunciation, to English Wiktionary.
- Kotiko has no servers of its own, no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Neither does this site.

Read the [privacy policy](/privacy/): it takes about two minutes.

## Meet Mira

<Image
  src={hero}
  width={960}
  alt="Mira, a black kitten with orange eyes, peeking over a purple moon while greetings in six languages float around her: Hola, Привет, Ciao, 你好, مرحبا, こんにちは."

Kotiko's mascot is a small black kitten. *Kotiko* bends котик (kotik), Russian for "kitty",
the way a learner bends a word they're still getting to know. And Mira's name is a clue: in
Spanish it means "look", in Latin "wonderful", in Russian "of the world" and "of peace".
[Read the whole story](/why/).
