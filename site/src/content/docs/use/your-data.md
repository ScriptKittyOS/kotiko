---
title: Your data
description: Back up, restore, export and delete your words.
---

Your words live in your browser unless you connect [your own server](/server/). Open your word
list (**Open your words** in the toolbar popup), then **Settings**, **Your data**.

## Back up and restore

- **Export backup (JSON)** saves one file with every word, to keep or to restore later. Tick
  **Include settings** to keep those too. Your AI key and your server's access key are never
  in it.
- **Import a backup…** shows what the file holds (new words, words to merge, words you changed
  since) before anything changes. Words you changed since the backup are kept.
- **Remind me to back up** makes the toolbar popup remind you once a month while your words are
  only in this browser.

## Share and study

- **Spreadsheet (CSV)** exports all words, or only the ones your current filter shows.
- **Anki cards** exports a deck per language for [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/). In Anki:
  File, Import, choose the file. Importing a newer file updates the same cards.

## Delete

- **Delete one word** from your word list, or with **Undo** right after adding it.
- **Delete everything…** removes your words, settings, keys and caches from this browser. It
  offers to download a backup first. Tick the boxes to also delete the words on your Kotiko
  server, and the settings your other browsers share through browser sync.
- **Uninstalling** Kotiko removes everything it keeps in this browser, except settings stored
  in your browser account by sync.

Kotiko can't delete what an AI service already received; that service's own policy applies.
