title: Choosing languages
description: Show, hide and focus on the languages you're learning, and set the languages you read.
---

## The languages you're learning

The toolbar popup shows a chip for every language you have words in, named in that language
(Русский, العربية, 日本語). Select a chip to show or hide that language on pages.

- **All of them:** when several languages have a word for the same thing, the page takes turns
  (one "thanks" becomes спасибо, the next 谢谢). Point at one to see the others.
- **Just one:** select **Focus on** the language (or press **F** on its chip). **Stop** ends it.
- **Some:** hide the ones you want to rest. **Show all** brings everything back.

A hidden language stays hidden until you show it again. A language you've just started always
shows.

## The languages you read

Kotiko swaps words only on pages in the languages you read, and keeps meanings in them. To
change them, open **Settings**, **Languages you read in**. You can have up to four. When you
visit a page in a language that isn't yours, the toolbar popup offers **I read … too**.

Kotiko's buttons and messages follow your browser's language where Kotiko has a translation.
**Settings**, **Kotiko's language** changes it; your words don't change.
