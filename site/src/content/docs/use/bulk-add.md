---
title: Adding a list of words
description: Paste or drop a whole list of words into Kotiko and check it before saving.
---

To add many words at once, for example a list a teacher handed out, open your word list
(**Open your words** in the toolbar popup) and select **Add words**. The welcome page's
"Have a list already?" link opens the same place.

1. Paste the list, one word per line, or drop a `.txt`, `.csv`, `.tsv` or `.json` file (up to
   5 MB). Each line can be:
   - `gato = cat` or `perro - dog`: a word and its meaning;
   - `спасибо (spasibo) = thanks`: with a romanization;
   - `mariposa`: just the word, for Kotiko to look up.
2. Check the table. Kotiko guesses which column is the word and which the meaning; select a
   column's heading to change it, or **Swap** them. Choose the language you're **Learning**
   and the language the **Meanings in** are in.
3. Untick any word you don't want. Lines marked **Needs a meaning** or **Kotiko will find the
   word** are looked up after saving, if you have a [word lookup service](/providers/).
4. Select **Add … words**.

You can add up to 5,000 words at a time. Words you already have are merged, not added twice.

Kotiko never supplies word lists of its own: every word is one you chose.
