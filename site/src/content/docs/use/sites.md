---
title: Sites and pages
description: Turn Kotiko off, pause it on one site, and the pages it leaves alone.
---

- **Swap words on pages** in the toolbar popup turns Kotiko on and off everywhere.
- **Pause on this site** turns it off on the site you're on. **Resume** turns it back on.

## Pages Kotiko leaves alone

- **Your browser's own pages**, such as settings and the new tab page, and extension stores:
  browsers don't let extensions change them.
- **Banks, payment pages, health portals, government and tax sites, and email you're
  writing**: Kotiko recognises these and stays away, and the toolbar popup says why. **Swap
  words here anyway** overrides it for that site.
- **Buttons, menus and form fields**: left alone by default, so controls read as you expect.
- **Pages in a language you don't read**: see [Choosing languages](/use/languages/).

If a page keeps undoing Kotiko's changes, Kotiko steps back on that page and says so in the
toolbar popup.

## What websites can see

The words Kotiko swaps in become part of the page, so the site's own scripts can see them. They
can't see your word list or the meanings. The [privacy policy](/privacy/#what-websites-can-see)
explains this.

## Keyboard

Select a swapped word and press **Alt+Shift+R** to open its card; **Tab** moves through it and
**Esc** closes it.
